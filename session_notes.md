# Curse of Strahd — Session Notes
# Chapter 6: Old Bonegrinder

Update this file before each session. Add new SCENE sections for every location
the party might reach. When the session is done, move the key events into
campaign_history.md and refresh this file for the next session.

**Opening Scene:** Old Bonegrinder — Ground Floor (Area O1)
**Tone:** Gothic horror / dark fairy tale. Hags are charming, not monstrous. Dread creeps; it does not announce itself.

---

## SESSION OVERVIEW

**Where we are:** The party is on the Svalich Road at night. A zombie horde
shepherded by a Barovian witch has driven them from the road. The only light
visible for miles is the lantern glow from Old Bonegrinder windmill above them.
Morgantha arranged this.

**What this chapter is about:** A pressure cooker. The hags are not attacking —
they are hosting. The horror is the hospitality. The party should leave feeling
they narrowly survived something that was never quite a fight.

**Key dramatic beats:**
- The warmth and smell of the kitchen after the terror of the road
- The wrongness that slowly accumulates: the smell under the pastries, the ichor barrel, Offalia's loom
- The discovery of the children
- Ireena's reaction to the children — she will not leave without them
- Whether anyone eats a dream pastry (and the addiction save that follows)
- The negotiation or the fight — or both

---

## SCENE: Old Bonegrinder — Exterior

### Description
A crumbling stone windmill on a hillock above the Svalich Road. Three storeys.
The great sails are still. A warm orange glow flickers from the ground-floor
windows. The hillock is encircled by the Megaliths — ancient standing stones,
older than the windmill, older perhaps than Barovia itself. Offerings have been
left at the base of several stones: bundles of hair, small bones, a child's shoe.

Something watches from the stones. It has no shape the characters can fix their
eyes on. It is simply a feeling of attention — vast, patient, amused.
(This is Ceithlenn of the Crooked Teeth. Do not name her yet.)

The zombie horde moans below on the road. The witch's eyes gleam in the fog.
The windmill door opens before anyone knocks. Morgantha is expecting them.

## SCENE: Area O1 — Ground Floor (Kitchen)

### Description
Warm. Cramped. The smell of baking hits first — rich, sweet, almost unbearably
inviting after the cold of the road. Wicker baskets crowd every corner, each
sealed with rough twine. A chicken coop sits against one wall, the birds
unnaturally still. A weathered cabinet painted with cheerful flowers stands
near the stairs; inside, rows of small bottles catch the firelight.

Near the stairs: an open barrel of tar-black substance. It looks like pitch.
It smells faintly wrong — sulphurous, with a sweetness beneath it that
doesn't belong. (Demonic ichor. Scrying font. DC 15 Arcana to suspect its use.)

The oven dominates the far wall. Morgantha works at it constantly, never
quite stopping, never quite turning her back on the room.

### The Cabinet (Potions)
Potions of Healing and Potions of Poison — visually identical, same bottle,
same colour. When a character drinks one, roll a d6 in secret: odd = poison
(2d4 poison damage, DC 13 Con or poisoned for 1 hour), even = healing (2d4+2).
Morgantha knows which is which. She does not volunteer this.

### The Ichor Barrel
Characters who examine it closely and succeed on DC 15 Arcana recognise demonic
ichor — a substance associated with scrying, binding, and infernal contracts.
Morgantha, if asked: "Oh that old thing, deary — keeps the rats away. Terrible
smell, I know, I know."

## SCENE: Area O2 — Middle Floor (Bella's Room)

### Description
Bella's domain. Cheerful chaos: cheap jewellery hanging from every nail, a
cracked mirror surrounded by candle stubs, a pile of colourful rags that might
be clothing. Bella herself is wherever is most inconvenient.

The smell of the kitchen does not quite reach here. Something else does:
copper, faint but persistent.

## SCENE: Area O3 — Upper Floor (Offalia's Room / The Cage)

### Description
Offalia's loom takes up half the room. The cloth on it is a muted grey-brown,
densely woven, oddly textured. Characters who examine it closely (Perception DC 15)
notice that the pattern is not abstract: there is a face in the weave. A human face.
Eyes closed, mouth slightly open. If the character makes DC 12 Wis save they do
not reach out to touch it; if they fail, their fingers brush it before they
realise what they are doing.

In the corner, behind a stack of bone-meal sacks: an iron cage bolted to the floor.
Inside it, two children.

### Freek (age 7)
Sits with his back against the bars, arms around Myrtle. He does not look up when
the door opens. He has learned that looking up leads to nothing good. If a character
crouches to his level and speaks quietly, he will look — and the wariness in his
eyes is the worst thing in the room. He asks one question before anything else:
"Are you going to eat us?"

### Myrtle (age 5)
Has her face pressed into Freek's shoulder. She is not crying — she ran out of
tears some time ago. When she finally looks up: "Can we go home?"
She asks this every time. She still believes the answer might change.

### The Cage
AC 19, 10 HP, immune to poison and psychic damage.
Thieves' Tools DC 15 to pick the lock.
Strength DC 23 to bend the bars.
The key is on Morgantha's person.

## NPCs

### Morgantha — Green Hag, Matriarch
**CR 3. HP 82. AC 17.**

**Disguise:** Stooped crone, seventies, apple-cheeked, bright-eyed. Warm woollen
shawl. Flour on her hands.

**Voice:** Unhurried. Melodious. Fond of "deary" and "my flower." Never raises it.

**Manner:** Permanently busy — always stirring, always wiping a surface, always
offering food. The hospitality is a form of control: a guest who is eating is a
guest who is not leaving.

**Compliments:** Scalpel-precise backhanders, delivered with total warmth:
- "What strength you have, deary — if only you could scrub the grease from your face."
- "Such brave eyes. Frightened eyes, but brave. That's almost touching."
- "You remind me of someone. Someone who came through here some years ago. He didn't
  leave, in the end. But you have a much nicer quality about you."

**On Ireena:** She will not harm her. Strahd's obsession is her insurance. She may say:
"Evil's mark is upon you, deary. I can smell it from here. Don't be embarrassed —
it's not your fault at all."

**On the children:** She does not acknowledge them unless directly asked. If asked:
"Oh, those are my grandchildren. Dreadful tempers, both of them. Best left alone."

**On the pastries:** She pushes them constantly. If refused: "Oh, but deary — you
look positively famished. Positively." If pressed about ingredients: "Family recipe.
Very old. I couldn't possibly."

**In combat:** She reveals her true face — violet and green flesh — by ripping her
human mask off and throwing it at the nearest adventurer (DC 12 Dex or frightened
until end of next turn). She prioritises crowd control: Hold Person on the most
dangerous fighter, Bestow Curse on the healer. She flees (Invisibility) when
reduced to 20 HP or fewer.

**Key phrases:** "Deary." "Famished." "My flower of a daughter." "Oh, couldn't leave
well enough alone."

---

### Bella Sunbane — Green Hag, Middle Daughter
**CR 3. HP 82. AC 17.**

**Disguise:** Loud, gregarious, emotionally immature. Cheap jewellery: silver choker
too tight for her throat, garish rings on every finger.

**Manner:** Overpowering warmth. She will squeeze arms, offer tea, promise to be best
friends. She is genuinely excited by the adventurers' arrival in the way a child is
excited by a new toy. She interrupts Morgantha constantly, to the crone's irritation.

**In combat:** Erratic and physical. She is the one most likely to abandon the plan
and simply start hitting things. If the coven breaks, she is the most dangerous
long-term: she will seek Strahd's patronage and raise an undead army.

---

### Offalia Wormwiggle — Green Hag, Youngest Daughter
**CR 3. HP 82. AC 17.**

**Disguise:** Plain. Shy. Will not meet anyone's eye. Smiles at the floor. Speaks
just above a whisper.

**Manner:** She confides a secret to any male PC Morgantha sends upstairs:
"Mother only wishes to marry me off because I disgust her so." She believes this.
She holds deep resentment of Bella. She is not performing vulnerability — it is real.

**In combat:** The floater. She can descend the stairs, climb the exterior, or drop
from a window to strike where the hags are outnumbered.

---

### Freek — Human Child, age 7
See Area O3 description above. He will not trust adults he does not know.
Exception: Ireena Kolyana. He recognises her from the village. If she is present
when the cage is opened, he goes to her immediately.

---

### Myrtle — Human Child, age 5
See Area O3 description above. She will cling to any adult who is kind to her.
She must not be left behind. If she is, Ireena will go back for her.

## GM NOTES — DO NOT REVEAL TO PLAYERS

**The hag coven's plan:**
Morgantha wants information more than blood. She knows the party is significant —
the Vistani have been talking. She wants to know what they are looking for and
whether they can be useful to her. The hospitality buys her time to assess them.

**The dream pastries:**
Made from the refined dreams of children (Freek and Myrtle among them). Eating
one: DC 16 Con save on next sleep or gain the flaw "I can't think straight without
a dream pastry." Morgantha has six in the oven. She will not confirm ingredients
under any circumstances.

**The witch outside:**
She is herding, not attacking. She will not enter the windmill. If the party tries
to flee back through the horde, the witch directs the zombies to block — not kill.
She wants them inside.

**Ceithlenn of the Crooked Teeth:**
The archfey the hags worship is present at the Megaliths. Any warlock or Feywild-
adjacent character feels her attention as a prickling at the back of the neck —
the sensation of being watched by something that finds you interesting. Do not
name her tonight. Let her be a feeling.

**If the party is defeated:**
Morgantha casts Spare the Dying (as a grandmotherly kiss to the forehead). She
wants prisoners and leverage, not corpses. Survivors wake bound in rope woven from
human hair, 1 HP each, cage unlocked. Freek and Myrtle are still there.

## DARK BARGAINS AVAILABLE

**Malison for Mercy**
In exchange for a dark curse, Morgantha spares a downed adventurer. Curse options:
loss of shadow / loss of an eye / rapid ageing 1d10 years / loss of darkvision /
loss of the ability to speak untruths / betrothal to Offalia.

**Casting the First Stone**
A geas-enforced three-part bargain:
1. Deliver a sealed letter to Henrik van der Voort in Vallaki.
2. Stand aside when comrades enter the coffin maker's home.
3. Cast the first stone at Baron Vallakovich's public execution.

**Portal to Castle Ravenloft**
In exchange for: Arabelle's blood / Khazan's staff of power / the identities of
the Keepers of the Feather / safe passage to the Amber Temple — Morgantha opens
a teleportation circle to the Brazier Room (Area K78).
