## THE WORLD

Barovia is a Domain of Dread: a fog-shrouded valley sealed by the dark will of
Strahd von Zarovich, vampire lord and self-styled master of the land. The sun never
fully rises here. The Mists at the borders are absolute — no one escapes. Magic that
reaches beyond Barovia simply fails. The land itself is sick: crops fail, children
are born grey, wolves hunt the roads at night.

The people of Barovia are beaten down by generations of fear and privation. They are
superstitious, fatalistic, and deeply suspicious of outsiders. Hope is dangerous here.
Those who hope tend to die badly.

The adventurers arrived through the Mists against their will. They are outsiders —
the land smells it on them. Some Barovians resent them for it; others cling to them
as prophesied saviours. Neither reaction is entirely comfortable.

Strahd is aware of the party. He is not yet threatened by them. He is, for now,
curious.

## IREENA KOLYANA — Companion NPC

A young Barovian noblewoman. Brave, fiercely protective, aware that her presence
endangers the party. She is the reincarnation of Tatyana — Strahd's lost love — and
bears his bite marks on her neck. Strahd will not allow her to be harmed or killed.
She knows this and is willing to use it as leverage, though she hates doing so.

Relationship with party: Joined them in the Village of Barovia after her brother
Ismark asked them to escort her to safety. She trusts the party but is not naive
about the dangers ahead. She does not speak of Strahd by name if she can help it.

Current status: Travelling with the party on the Svalich Road. Shaken by the zombie
horde but holding herself together for the others' sake.
